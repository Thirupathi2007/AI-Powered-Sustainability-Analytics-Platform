System Architecture & ML Methodology

Designed with production software patterns, modular micro-services, and seamless model replacement capabilities.

Project Mission & Relevance

Corporate sustainability is undergoing a seismic shift: regulatory frameworks such as the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD), CSRD, and SEC Climate Disclosures require verifiable, quantitative emission telemetry. Traditional retrospective audits cost tens of thousands of dollars and provide zero predictive modeling.

VerdaMetric AI solves this by marrying GHG Protocol Scope 1-3 carbon accounting formulas with supervised machine learning ensembles. Organizations can simulate capital interventions (such as rooftop solar PPAs, HVAC variable frequency drive retrofits, and fleet electrification) and verify their projected ESG tier before deploying capital.

Drop-In Model & Dataset Replacement Guide

How to plug your own trained ML model and custom dataset into this website.

This system was engineered specifically so that any developer or data scientist can swap out the ML model and dataset without rewriting frontend code.

# Step 1: Place your custom trained model and scaler in the models/ directory: models/sustainability_model.pkl # Your custom scikit-learn / XGBoost model models/scaler.pkl # Your fitted StandardScaler or preprocessor # Step 2: Place your custom dataset in the data/ directory: data/sustainability_data.csv # Your CSV dataset file # Step 3: Run the automated training pipeline anytime to re-generate metrics: py train_model.py # Step 4: Restart the Flask server: py run.py

Documentation Model Integration

How to Replace Dataset & ML Model

You can seamlessly replace the built-in model with your own trained .pkl file and dataset:

1. Where to put your dataset:
Drop your CSV file into: data/sustainability_data.csv.
The Dataset page will immediately load and profile your records.
2. Where to put your trained ML model:
Drop your trained model file into: models/sustainability_model.pkl
And your fitted scaler into: models/scaler.pkl
3. Re-generating evaluation metrics:
Run py train_model.py in the terminal. It automatically computes accuracy, ROC curves, confusion matrices, and exports models/metrics.json for the Model Performance charts.
